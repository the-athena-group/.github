# The Athena Group

**Humanity in every system.**

This is the technology home of [The Athena Group, LLC](https://www.athenaplace.com). It's where the firm's tech division builds and shares the tools, workflows, and knowledge that support our consulting work. [BitBarrel, LLC](https://bitbarrel.io) supports and maintains this work on Athena's behalf.

## Who we are

The Athena Group cultivates the capacity for transformational change in self, systems, and society.

We're a women- and minority-owned, for-purpose consulting firm headquartered in Olympia, Washington. Since 1999, The Athena Group has completed 750+ contracts with 275+ government, business, and community-based organizations. We're becoming an anti-racist, self-organizing enterprise, and we work with a mindset of shifting from "power over" to "power with," inside our firm and with our clients.

Our work rests on three pillars:

- **Self: Inner leadership development.** Coaching, training, and assessments that help people tap into their inner wisdom and keep learning.
- **Systems: Organizational health and change.** Facilitation, strategic planning, change management, and team support for organizations facing complex change.
- **Society: Community engagement and facilitation.** Inclusive public engagement that centers the voices of those most impacted.

## Why a tech division

Technology change is people change. Our [technology modernization](https://www.athenaplace.com/technology-modernization) practice helps state and local leaders guide modernization so systems are adopted, leaders are supported, and public trust is strengthened.

We hold ourselves to the same standard. Here, we explore how technology, including AI, can support our people and our clients without losing the human side of the work. We aim to:

- Build practical tools that make our consulting work clearer and more consistent.
- Keep what we learn organized, so knowledge stays with the firm and is easy to find.
- Test new technology on ourselves first, with care for ethics, trust, and adoption.

## Our values

We bring our values of curiosity, awareness, connection, equity, and wholeness to all the work we do, including the code we write.
