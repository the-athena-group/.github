# The Athena Group

**Humanity in every system.**

This is the technology home of [The Athena Group, LLC](https://www.athenaplace.com). It's where our tech division builds the tools our partners use to write proposals and find what Athena already knows. [BitBarrel, LLC](https://bitbarrel.io) supports and maintains this work on Athena's behalf.

## Who we are

The Athena Group cultivates the capacity for transformational change in self, systems, and society.

We're a women- and minority-owned consulting firm headquartered in Olympia, Washington. Since 1999, we've completed 750+ contracts with 275+ government, business, and community-based organizations.

We're also becoming an anti-racist, self-organizing firm. Our partners share decisions instead of passing them up a chain of command. We call it moving from "power over" to "power with," and we bring it to our work with clients too.

Our work rests on three pillars:

- **Self: Inner leadership development.** We help people notice their own patterns, grow as leaders, and keep learning, through coaching, training, and assessments.
- **Systems: Organizational health and change.** We help organizations work through complex change with facilitation, strategic planning, change management, and team support.
- **Society: Community engagement and facilitation.** We host hard public conversations so the people most affected by a policy help shape it.

## Why a tech division

Technology change is people change. Our [technology modernization](https://www.athenaplace.com/technology-modernization) practice helps state and local leaders guide modernization so people actually use the new systems, leaders get support along the way, and public trust grows.

We hold ourselves to the same standard. We're still learning how technology, including AI, can support our partners and clients without losing the human side of the work. So we try new tools on ourselves first. Here, we:

- Build tools that give our partners more time with clients and less time on paperwork.
- Keep what we learn organized, so knowledge stays with the firm and is easy to find.
- Pay attention to ethics, trust, and whether people actually adopt what we build.

## Our values

We bring our values of curiosity, awareness, connection, equity, and wholeness to all the work we do, including the code we write.
